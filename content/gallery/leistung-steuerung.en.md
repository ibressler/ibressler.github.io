I develop and configure control systems for custom and prototype machines,
as well as for older equipment with outdated or missing controls. This
includes selecting and sizing the control and drive technology, configuring
axes and kinematics — including non-standard types of motion — and
commissioning the machine through to a ready-to-operate handover. Where the
mechanics are worth keeping but the control system is no longer
maintainable, I replace it with a current system (modernisation/retrofit).

This makes it possible to operate machines reliably even when no standard
control system is available, and to keep existing equipment in use rather
than replacing it.

**For:** workshops, university workshops, machine and special-purpose
machine builders, and operations with sound mechanics but an outdated
control system.

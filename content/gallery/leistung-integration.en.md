I integrate production, test and measurement instruments into existing
software environments and automate the acquisition, storage and analysis
of measurement data. Instruments are connected via common interfaces such
as network, USB, serial connections, and others. This removes the need to
transfer readings by hand; the data is available in a structured,
consistent form and ready for immediate analysis.

This reduces sources of error, saves time and establishes the basis for
consistent digitalisation in laboratory and testing environments.

**For:** laboratories, research, development and testing teams, and small
manufacturers with test benches and measurement technology.
